# Privacy Policy — فہمِ قرآن (Quran Asaan)

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

This app shows the Quranic word cards of the book **آسان قرآنی قطعے** (Dr Tanveer Qasim), one word per screen.

**We do not collect any personal data.**

- The app has no account, sign-in or registration.
- The app does not use the internet. All pages are stored inside the app.
- The app does not use advertising, analytics or tracking.
- The app does not ask for any permissions (camera, location, contacts, storage, etc.).
- The only thing the app saves is the number of the last page you read, and it is kept on your own phone. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

No data is shared with anyone.

## Contact

For questions about this app or this policy, contact the developer through
https://github.com/MSM-official/Quran-Asaan/issues
